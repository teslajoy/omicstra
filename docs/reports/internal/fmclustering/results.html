Recommended fusion method

R4 · Cross-attention alignment

R4 is the only run that keeps tile-level H&E detail. All other methods mean-pool the seven tile vectors before projection. R4 lets the ST representation attend over individual tile tokens - the morphological context decides which tiles matter.

R4 · InfoNCE cross-attention
ST queries attend over 7 H&E tile tokens (center spot + 6 spatial neighbours). The molecular representation selects the most relevant morphological patches at each niche before projection to 512d shared space. Only the H&E side enters at prediction time.

What the AUC measures

ROC curves - ground truth is spatial co-registration

The retrieval task is a binary classification problem. Each niche is a matched pair: an H&E tile region centred on the same 100 µm spot as its ST observation, from the same physical 16 µm tissue section. Positive pair: same spatial coordinate. Negative pair: drawn from a different subarray of a held-out patient. The classifier is the cosine similarity between the two embeddings in the shared 512d space. AUC = 1.0 is perfect separation; AUC = 0.5 is random. B3 (AUC 0.44) is sub-chance - raw modality spaces are anti-correlated before alignment; a matched pair looks less similar than a mismatched one in the raw space.

Curves are binormal approximations from measured AUC scalars (d' = √2 · Φ-1(AUC)). Actual per-pair score distributions are in runs/tnbc-92_v3/eval/H1/. Five key methods shown; R2/R5/R6 sit between R1 and B2.

prior art - same evaluation protocol discovered independently in 2025-2026
spEMO Liu et al. 2026, Nature Biomedical Engineering. Introduces "multi-modal alignment" as a benchmark task: how well do pathology FMs retrieve complementary ST information? Identical to this evaluation frame. Finds late-fusion surpasses single-modality on spatial domain identification and disease prediction.
HESCAPE Gindra et al. 2025, ICCV Workshop. Large-scale cross-modal contrastive pretraining benchmark across 6 gene panels, 54 donors. Finds ST encoder is the primary determinant of alignment quality - consistent with our Novae platform-mismatch result (H&E ARI vs mc 0.093 > ST 0.033). Also finds contrastive pretraining improves retrieval but degrades direct expression prediction - the same H1/H2 trade-off we observe.
STORM Xiang et al. 2026, arXiv. Hierarchical FM trained on 1.2M spatially resolved profiles + matched H&E across 18 organs. Platform-agnostic (Visium/Xenium/CosMX). Tests immunotherapy response prediction (23 cohorts, 7,245 patients). Shares the shared-space-from-multiple-modalities design but is a foundation model retrain, not an alignment-and-route protocol.
What omicstra adds: (1) evaluation on a tissue/platform not in any training set (original ST 100 µm spots); (2) cross-attention tile-level fusion vs pooled late fusion; (3) patient-held-out cross-subarray evaluation; (4) H3 pathway CCA as an interpretability gate; (5) evidence-based routing that declines when evidence is absent.

Key findings

Five results that reframe the question

01
The untrained representation wins the TLS task H2-B.2 · structural coherence
Frozen Virchow2 features + CCA (no alignment training) predicts the 30-gene TLS score at median r 0.70 across all 286 leave-three-patients-out splits. Nine of ten trained alignment methods perform worse. The signal was already in the foundation model - alignment rearranged the space, it didn't add information. This is why untrained controls belong in the evaluation contract, not in someone's good intentions.
02
TLS is two signals that route to different methods H2-B · structural coherence
The categorical pathologist label (238 annotated niches, 23/92 patients) routes to late-fusion contrastive. The continuous 30-gene score (every niche with counts) routes to morphology-preserving methods including untrained projection. Same biological structure, two operationalizations, two different recommended methods. "Which method is best for TLS?" is underspecified until you say which measurement you mean.
03
Annotation quality is the dominant term in several results H1 · retrieval H2 · coherence
Retrieval is best in lymphoid nodule (FOSCTTM 0.067) and worst in tumor (0.161). The tumor label pools invasive nests, in situ disease, and the tumor-immune interface - four distinct molecular states - into one class with no single biology to align to. The vocabulary, not the method, determines the ceiling. Median spot-label purity is 59%; only 14.8% of spots are >95% one category.
04
No single method wins all objectives - routing is the deliverable H1 H2 H3
R4 cross-attention wins H1 retrieval and H3 cross-patient pathway transfer. R1/R6 late-fusion win H2 tissue-state clustering. CCA is contraindicated for biology/patient separation. The trade-off is the finding, not a failure. A system that routes by question and declines when the evidence contraindicates a method is what justified building the evaluation infrastructure in the first place.
05
Alignment is geometric, not informational H2-A · coherence H3 · interpretability
ARI rises ~50% over raw H&E under the best-performing runs. The linear probe moves 1.2 points against a majority-class floor of 0.216. Alignment rearranged the space so that ordinary clustering could recover what was already encoded - it did not add new decodable information. The smaller claim is the true one, and it has a cleaner implication: the next lever is a finer biological vocabulary, not a better model.
